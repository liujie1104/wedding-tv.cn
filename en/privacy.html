Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 14, 2026 · Back to Home

wedding-tv.cn ("we", "us", or "our") respects and protects your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information across our free wedding utilities, interactive live wedding wall, and cultural guides.

Active Operational Status (September 14, 2026):

wedding-tv.cn is committed to privacy by design. We do not load Google Analytics, Google AdSense ad-serving scripts, or third-party behavioral profiling trackers. Our pages contain a publisher account meta tag solely for AdSense site ownership verification without executing advertising scripts. Cloudflare Web Analytics is used for aggregate performance metrics without cookies or cross-site tracking. Most tools (timeline builder, countdown poster, checklists) process data entirely within your local browser.

1. Information We Process

a) Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Email Correspondence: When contacting us via email for feedback, corrections, or support, we receive your email address and any message content you provide.
  • Local Browser Inputs: Wedding dates, partner names, timeline schedules, and budget allocations are stored locally in your browser (via localStorage). These inputs are never uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly trigger an online sharing or AI generation feature.
  • Digital Wedding Invitations: When you publish an online invitation, couple names, date, venue, personal greeting, and photo references are saved to Cloudflare KV to render the public link. Anyone with the URL can view these public invitation details.
  • Live Wedding Wall Messages: When creating a room, couple names, wedding date, venue note, and moderation settings are stored. When guests submit blessings, their chosen nickname, table number/identity, message, and timestamp are saved. Messages are retained for 24 hours and room metadata expires after 7 days. Host moderation is enabled by default to prevent spam.
  • AI Features: When using AI vow writing, toast generation, or love story expansion, the prompt details you submit are securely transmitted to the AI inference provider (Alibaba DashScope, Google Gemini, or Cloudflare Workers AI) to generate the response. We do not maintain persistent user profiles or sell your text.

b) Automatically Collected Operational Data

  • Hosting & Security Telemetry: Delivered via Cloudflare's global edge network, standard technical server logs (IP address, browser user-agent, request timestamp, URL) may be processed transiently to deliver web assets, protect against DDoS attacks, and mitigate abuse.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: Privacy-first analytics script measuring page load times, country-level aggregate metrics, and device categories without using cookies or persistent identifiers.
  • API Rate Limiting & Abuse Defense: To safeguard free AI APIs from automated scraping, IP addresses are transformed using a salted one-way hash (plain IP is never stored) and kept in Cloudflare KV for up to 48 hours solely to track daily request quotas.

2. How We Use Information

We process information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To deliver core wedding utilities (invitation hosting, live big-screen interaction, and AI writing).
  • To prevent abusive traffic, denial of service attacks, and API exploitation.
  • To answer user support and content takedown requests.
  • To maintain server stability and site performance.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Cloudflare: Provides DNS, CDN edge delivery, KV storage for invitations/wall messages, and Web Analytics.
  • Alibaba DashScope / Qwen: Processes selected AI text generation requests.
  • Google Gemini: Secondary model provider for text generation and image comprehension tasks.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI: Model provider for serverless AI inference.
  • Static CDN Dependencies: QR code libraries are loaded from public CDNs (jsDelivr, cdnjs). User QR data is rendered locally on your device and never sent to external QR generators unless client-side rendering completely fails.

4. Data Retention & Expiration

  • Live Wedding Wall: Individual blessing messages expire automatically after 24 hours. Room configurations expire after 7 days. Room hosts can manually delete all messages at any time.
  • Digital Invitations & Photos: New invitations created after August 2026 are scheduled to expire automatically after 365 days. Early deletion requests can be submitted anytime.
  • Rate Limiting Keys: Salted hash counters expire after 48 hours.
  • Browser Data: Stored locally until you click "Clear All" or wipe your browser cache.

5. Your Rights & GDPR / CCPA Compliance

Depending on your location, you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Inquire about what data is stored relating to your invitation or wedding wall room.
  • Deletion: Request immediate removal of published invitations or uploaded wedding images.
  • Opt-Out: Our site does not sell personal data, does not serve targeted ads, and does not conduct cross-site tracking.

We implement modern security practices including end-to-end SSL/TLS encryption, salted hashing for rate-limiting, strict input sanitization against XSS, and automated content expiration.
