    First Time Using at a Wedding Reception

    How to connect laptop and screen?

    Connect your laptop to the venue screen or projector via HDMI. Ask venue AV staff to verify signal source and aspect ratio (16:9). Press F11 in your browser for fullscreen, and ensure the title, floating bubbles, and QR code are completely visible.

    What happens when guests scan the QR code?

    The QR code opens the mobile blessing page. Guests enter a nickname and congratulations. The host receives messages in the moderation queue and clicks 'Approve' before they appear on screen. No photo upload or guest account needed.

    How does the host maintain control?

    The private host management link is separate from the guest link. Hosts can pause display, delete inappropriate entries, or temporarily stop accepting new wishes. The on-screen lucky draw picks randomly from unique approved nicknames.

    What about network drops and data expiry?

    Both laptop and guest phones require an internet connection. If connection drops, the screen displays a retry status and recovers automatically. Rooms expire 7 days after creation, and wishes clear 24 hours after submission. Export before the event ends.

    Printable QR Table Cards & Keepsake Exports

    Generate high-resolution PNG QR table cards inside the room to print and place on guest tables. You can also export a 20-message blessing memorial image or a full CSV list. All images and files are generated directly in your browser without third-party exposure.
