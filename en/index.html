Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about using our wedding tools and live screen.

Are these wedding tools completely free?

Yes. All core utilities, including the live screen interaction, timeline builder, and invitation generator, are completely free without any hidden subscriptions.

Do guests need to download an app to send blessings?

No. Guests simply scan the QR code using any smartphone browser camera. They fill in their nickname and message, and submit directly.

How does host moderation work on the live screen?

When you create a room, you receive a private host management key. In the host dashboard, you can approve or delete incoming wishes before they appear on screen.

Can international guests participate at cross-cultural weddings?

Yes. The guest blessing page supports both English and Chinese. Guests can toggle language preferences at the top of the mobile page.
