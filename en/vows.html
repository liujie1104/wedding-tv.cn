Tips for Writing & Delivering Unforgettable Vows

How long should wedding vows be?

The sweet spot for ceremony vows is typically between 1.5 to 2.5 minutes (roughly 150 to 250 spoken words). This gives you enough time to share meaningful stories and make solemn promises without losing guest engagement.

How to use this AI draft

Think of this tool as your personal speechwriting brainstorming partner. Take the generated draft, infuse it with your inside jokes, your partner's exact nicknames, and read it aloud twice in front of a mirror before printing your ceremony vow books.

What structure works best?

A classic three-part formula: 1) What you love and admired when you first met; 2) A defining shared memory that proved your partnership; 3) 3-4 specific, loving promises for your future together.

Pacing and Delivery Advice

Speak slowly and deliberately—most speakers naturally rush when nervous on stage. Pause after meaningful moments to let the emotions land. Have a printed pocket vow booklet or card ready instead of relying on a smartphone screen, ensuring your photos look timeless.
