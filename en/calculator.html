Negotiation & Review Checklist

  • Use this estimate as a breakdown baseline to ask vendors to explain specific pricing differences line by line.
  • Confirm whether the quote includes raw footage delivery, audio backup, color grading passes, and overtime hourly rates.
  • If booking wedding live-streaming, verify dedicated mobile bonding hardware, backup internet, and local high-res recording.
  • Ensure same-day edit (SDE) delivery hours and reception projection requirements are clearly stated in writing.
  • Deposit terms should be explicitly tied to milestones with a clear cancellation and postponement clause.
