Timeline Planning Principles

1. Buffer Time is Essential: Always allocate 15-20 minutes of buffer before the ceremony and between photoshoots. Things running 10 minutes late is completely normal.

2. Designate a Coordinator: Never make the bride or groom responsible for tracking time on the day. Assign your schedule to the bridal party, best man, or wedding planner.

3. Vendor Arrival Times: Hair & makeup and photographers should arrive 2-3 hours before the first scheduled photo or first-look session.
