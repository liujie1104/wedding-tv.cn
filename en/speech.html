Wedding Speech Etiquette & Timing Guide

How long should a reception toast be?

Aim for 2.5 to 4 minutes (roughly 300-500 words). Speeches that run longer than 5 minutes risk losing guest attention and throwing off dinner service timing.

Golden rules for Best Man & Maid of Honor

1) Introduce yourself and your relationship to the couple; 2) Share a funny, tasteful story showing their true character; 3) Speak about how they changed for the better after meeting their spouse; 4) Ask everyone to raise their glasses to the happy couple.
