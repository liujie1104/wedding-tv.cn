How to Use Your Planning Checklist

This tool organizes essential milestones into five distinct phases. Checkbox progress is saved in your browser's local storage so you can return anytime. The tool does not require an account or upload private data.

  • Do not enter sensitive financial account numbers or personal identification details.
  • Specific contracts, legal marriage requirements, and local vendor fees should always be verified directly with venues, vendors, and local civil registry offices.

The Four Pillars of Wedding Tasks

Categorize your items into four pillars: Contracts & Deadlines, Vendors & Bookings, Logistics & Decor, and Ceremony & Vows. Assigning a clear responsible person to each item prevents misunderstandings on the wedding day.
