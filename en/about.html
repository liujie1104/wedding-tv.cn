About wedding-tv.cn

A dedicated, privacy-first web suite offering free planning utilities, interactive wedding screens, and cultural guides for modern couples and wedding vendors worldwide.

Who We Are

wedding-tv.cn was established out of hands-on experience in wedding day coordination, video production, and banquet AV technology. We noticed that couples frequently struggle with scattered checklists, non-transparent vendor pricing, and fragmented day-of schedules, while wedding vendors spend unnecessary hours repeatedly explaining technical run-of-shows and AV logistics.

Our solution is to organize common wedding preparation workflows into instant, client-side web tools that work effortlessly on laptops, projectors, and mobile phones without mandatory registrations or paywalls.

Our Core Mission

  • Empower Couples with Free Tools: Provide practical tools like live interactive screen projections, day-of run-of-show generators, smart checklists, wedding countdowns, and budget calculators to minimize stress and financial uncertainty.
  • Privacy by Design: Most of our utilities run and store data entirely in your local browser storage. For features that require cloud coordination (like live screen blessing walls and published invitations), data is automatically cleared with strict expiration lifecycles.
  • Promote Industry Transparency: Help couples understand camera coverage models, same-day edit expectations, live-streaming logistics, and vendor contract terms so everyone shares a transparent foundation for communication.
  • Cross-Cultural Ceremony Guidance: Present verified cultural context for multi-cultural and destination weddings, distinguishing genuine heritage customs from unverified commercial myths.

Our Free Tools

  • 📺 Live Wedding Blessing Screen: Turn any screen into an interactive guest wall. Guests scan a QR code to submit warm wishes; the host moderates content in real-time, runs fair prize draws, and exports printable table cards.
  • 💌 Digital Wedding Invitation Maker: Design elegant vertical mobile invitations and 9:16 Save-the-Date social posters featuring music, custom greetings, and one-click calendar (.ics) integration.
  • ⏱️ Wedding Day Timeline Builder: Plan hour-by-hour run-of-show schedules with preset morning reception, evening banquet, and Western ceremony templates. Export to PNG or CSV.
  • 💰 Photo & Video Budget Calculator: Interactive pricing estimator that models realistic budget ranges across camera crew setups, duration, live streams, and cinematic highlight films.
  • 📋 Smart Wedding Planning Checklist: Step-by-step milestone checklist covering 6 months, 3 months, 1 month, 1 week, and wedding day execution.
  • 🎤 AI Vows & Toast Inspiration: Guided wording tools to help speakers craft heartfelt vows, best man toasts, maid of honor speeches, and parents' blessings.
